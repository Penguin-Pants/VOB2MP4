# Claw Cut brand guidelines

## Identity
Claw Cut = two separated metallic film pieces intersected by a sharp diagonal red blade. The red blade signifies a precise split, not danger or an error state. Use the symbol for VOB2MP4 only.

## Inherited from Dr. Claw Development
Shadow Black `#0B0B0B`, Charcoal `#111111`, Villain Red `#E10606`, Steel Gray `#4A4A4A`, Metallic Silver `#8F959C` and Highlight White `#EDEDED`; strong angular form; restrained industrial styling; readable high-contrast UI.

## VOB2MP4-specific
Filmstrip halves, diagonal blade and `VOB2MP4` custom path wordmark. Red emphasizes the numeral 2 and blade. Cut-marker green and job-progress blue are functional, not shared publisher-brand tokens.

## Safe use
- Maintain proportions and at least 8% clear space around mark in promotional contexts.
- Prefer dark icon for Windows. Use monochrome or light variant only when environment requires it.
- At 16 or 32 px use `icon-small.svg`; at higher sizes use `icon-master.svg`.
- Do not add neon glow, render a traced television character or place another Dr. Claw product mark inside this icon.
- Never infer usability by color alone. Keep visible text, shape and focus indicators.

## Custom vector wordmark
`wordmark-master.svg` contains geometric vector strokes; no system or licensed font is needed to render the wordmark. At normal UI sizes a readable font-based product name is acceptable. Do not use this stylized lettering for body text.

## Approval
Concept 01 explicitly approved 2026-10-19. Final Windows look and contrast require platform acceptance tests. The source icon is original vector geometry, not a raster tracing of the preliminary concept board.
